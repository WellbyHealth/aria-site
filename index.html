The problem

Your practice’s money runs through five vendors and lands on you.

A bookkeeper, a biller, a bank, a payroll company and a CPA you see at tax time. Nobody owns the whole picture, so it leaks in the same five places.

  • Denials nobody appeals

    Your biller works the claims they have time for. The rest quietly expire.

  • Insurers paying below contract

    Nobody checks each payment against your contracted rate, so short pays go unnoticed.

  • Bills paid late or twice

    Invoices arrive by email, portal and paper, and some get paid twice while others pick up late fees.

  • Books a month behind

    You make hiring and spending calls on numbers from six weeks ago.

  • Cash you can’t see coming

    Payroll lands on a Friday and you check the balance on Thursday night.

Questions

What owners ask us.

Still curious? Email us and you’ll hear back from a person.

Do I have to replace my bookkeeper, biller or CPA?

No. We can take over the work, or run it alongside the people you trust. Most practices keep their CPA for taxes; we hand them clean books every month.

Who approves payments?

You do. Nothing is paid and no account is opened without your sign-off. Approvals take a few minutes a week from your phone.

Are you a bank or a lender?

No. We’re a finance service. Bank accounts, cards, financing and payroll come from licensed partners, subject to their approval and terms, and we manage them for you.

How do you handle patient information?

Billing follow-up touches patient data, so we sign a business associate agreement with your practice before we start and only work in systems that meet HIPAA requirements.

What does it cost?

A flat monthly fee based on what you hand us, starting at $2,500 a month. Our first practices get a 90-day pilot at half price. See pricing for details.

How long does it take to start?

About two weeks: a checkup, a kickoff call, access to your accounts, then we take over one area at a time.
