Questions

What agencies ask us.

Does Kith replace our CRM or care software?

No. Kith is built to work alongside WellSky, AxisCare and similar systems, and to read the referral emails your team already gets. Your team keeps working the way it does today.

Who is Kith for?

Home care agencies first, private pay included, plus home health, hospice and senior living teams that grow through referral relationships.

How do you handle patient information?

We’re building Kith to meet HIPAA requirements. We’ll share our security practices and sign a business associate agreement before any patient information is connected.

Pricing isn’t final. Agencies that join the waitlist will see pricing in writing first, and early agencies get founding pricing.
