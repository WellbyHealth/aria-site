The problem

Practice spending is scattered, and no card was built for it.

In most independent practices, supplies, lab fees, drugs and software end up on a mix of the owner's personal card, a bank card and paper invoices. Staff borrow the owner's card to place orders. Receipts go missing. At month end, someone matches statements to invoices by hand.

General business cards weren't designed around a practice. They don't know a dental lab from a restaurant, they can't limit a card to medical supplies, and they don't help with the invoices that make up much of a practice's spending.

The owner pays for it twice: in hours every week spent on bills, receipts and reconciliation, and in cash tied up while insurers take their time and supplier bills come due.

Aria starts with the card because it's where the mess begins. Everything a practice buys flows through it, so getting it right makes the rest of the back office easier.

What will Aria cost?

Pricing isn't final. Practices on the waitlist will see pricing and terms in writing before applying for anything, and joining the waitlist is free with no obligation.

When will the card be available?

We're opening to practices in small groups ahead of a wider launch. Joining the waitlist is how you get an invitation; we'll email you when your group opens.

Who can apply?

Aria is built for independent healthcare and animal care businesses in the United States of any size, from a single provider to multi-location groups. Applications will be reviewed by our card-issuing partner.

Will I need a personal guarantee or a credit check?

Underwriting criteria, including whether a personal guarantee or credit check is required, will be shared before applications open.

What will the rewards be?

You'll earn 5% back on supplies ordered through the Aria marketplace and 1% back on all other card spending. Full rewards terms, including how rewards are redeemed, will be published before launch.

Do we have to switch banks?

No. The card works alongside your current bank. You can pay the card balance from your existing account.

Which practice and accounting systems do you work with?

We're building connections to common accounting software and practice management systems, prioritized by what waitlist practices use. Tell us yours when you join.

How does Aria use AI?

Aria AI does the busywork: reading receipts and invoices, coding transactions, matching payments and flagging anything unusual. It suggests and you approve. Nothing is paid, moved or changed without a person signing off. We'll publish exactly how we use AI and handle data before launch.

How will you keep our data secure?

We're building Aria to handle sensitive practice and patient-adjacent information with care, including encryption and strict access controls. We'll publish our security practices before launch.

Is Aria a bank?

No. Aria is a financial technology company. Card, banking and lending services will be provided by licensed partner institutions, which we'll name before launch.
