Fair questions.

Talk to us if we missed yours.

What will Aria cost?

Pricing isn't final. Waitlist practices will see it first, before committing to anything.

How long does setup take?

We're designing setup to take one sitting. We'll give you a realistic timeline when we invite you in.

Which practice systems do you work with?

We're building connections to widely used practice systems, prioritized by what waitlist practices use.

How do you keep our data secure?

Encryption, strict access controls, and a design built to healthcare privacy and card payment standards. We'll publish details before launch.

Do we have to switch banks?

No. Start with payments or the card and keep your current bank.

Is Aria a bank?

No. Aria is a financial technology company. Banking and lending services will be provided by licensed partner institutions.
