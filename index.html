The problem

You run a practice. You also run a patchwork.

Care happens today. The money shows up later, in pieces, across tools that were never built for a medical office.

Bank loginBusiness cardCard readerPayrollAccountingInsurer portalsSupply vendorsSpreadsheet

  • Insurers pay late. Bills don't wait.

    Reimbursement arrives weeks or months after the visit. Payroll and rent are due now.

  • Balances walk out the door.

    Once a patient leaves, what they owe gets much harder to collect.

  • Nothing talks to anything.

    Bank, card, payroll, and accounting sit in separate tools. Your office manager connects them by hand.

  • You pay full price for supplies.

    A small practice buys alone, without the purchasing power larger groups have.

  • Your tools weren't made for you.

    Most cards and finance apps don't know what a claim, a copay, or an insurer payment is.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Have one we missed? Talk to us.

What will Aria cost?

Pricing isn't final yet. Practices on the waitlist will see it first, in plain terms, before committing to anything. Joining the waitlist is free and carries no obligation.

How long does setup take?

We're designing setup to be something you finish in a sitting, not a project you manage. We'll share a realistic timeline for your practice when we invite you in.

Which practice systems do you work with?

We're building connections to widely used practice management and billing systems, and choosing the order based on what waitlist practices use. Tell us yours when we reach out and we'll let you know where it stands.

How do you keep our data secure?

Aria handles patient and payment information, so security is part of the design from the start. That means encryption, strict access controls, and building to healthcare privacy and card payment standards. We'll publish our security practices before launch.

Do we have to switch banks?

No. You can start with patient payments or the card and keep your current bank. Move more over only if and when it makes sense for you.

Is Aria a bank?

No. Aria is a financial technology company. Banking and lending services will be provided by licensed partner institutions.
